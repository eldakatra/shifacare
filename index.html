    Why ShifaCare?

    Real Problems. Practical Ideas.

    Four everyday difficulties in healthcare, and how technology could help.

    Problem

    Long Waiting Lines

    Solution

    Online appointment booking helps organize visits and reduce unnecessary waiting.

    Problem

    Unclear Hospital Crowds

    Solution

    Crowd indicators and estimated waiting times help patients plan their visits.

    Problem

    Difficulty Accessing Healthcare

    Solution

    Hospital search and specialty filters make it easier to find appropriate services.

    Problem

    High Upfront Medicine Costs

    Solution

    Transparent installment options can help eligible patients spread costs over time.
